Vanilla where it matters

Loot table, economy and balance are untouched. What you learned on any other vanilla server still applies here.

  • No traders
  • No kits, no starter gear, no donor gear
  • No pay-to-win — nothing on this server is for sale
  • No bots

If you are looking for a full 1PP vanilla server and the big ones are capped out, this is the same thing with room in it.

The one addition is cosmetic

BZA PaintMod is our own mod. It changes how your gear looks and nothing else.

Per-part painting

Buttstock, handguard, receiver, magazine — each painted separately. Clothing and armour too.

Patterns are earned

Find a Pattern Paint Can, learn the pattern off its label, keep it for the rest of the wipe.

Camo is the endgame

Military Paint Cans only spawn in Tier 3/4 military zones and at the airfield. That hunt is the long goal.

Painting is free for every player. The mod on the Steam Workshop — you do not need to install it by hand, the launcher pulls it when you join.

Server rhythm

  • Hosted in Warsaw — low ping across Central and Eastern Europe, Germany and the Nordics
  • Day ×6 / night ×4 — short but real nights
  • Restarts at 02:00, 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00 (Kyiv time, UTC+3) — loot respawns, so joining right after one is worth it
  • Wipes every 6 weeks

Community

Everything happens on our Discord — announcements, wipe dates, paint jobs players show off, and the fastest way to reach an admin.
