The one addition is cosmetic

BZA PaintMod is our own mod, built for this server. It changes how your gear looks and nothing else — no stat is touched, no item is added to the loot economy that gives you an edge in a fight.

Per-part painting

Buttstock, handguard, receiver, muzzle device, magazine — each painted separately. Clothing and armour too.

Patterns are earned

Find a Pattern Paint Can, learn the stencil off its label, keep it for the rest of the wipe.

Camo is the endgame

Military Paint Cans come out of high-tier military, the airfield, gas zones and four fixed caches. 25 combinations to collect.

Codes drop in Discord

Events and giveaways hand out promo codes — press ESC in game, type the code in, wear a colour loot cannot give you.

What you start with: four colours and one stencil. Everything past that is earned — four more stencils from cans you find, twenty-five camo combinations from the military hunt, and extra colours from event codes in Discord. That is the whole point of it. Every colour and pattern you can get.

Founder colour — first five only

The first five players to complete a full advanced pattern — all five earned schemes of any one of Pixel, Thicket, Tiger, Blotch or Hex — keep an exclusive colour, #A056F5, permanently. Nobody else on the server will ever have it.

It works on weapons and armour with the standard patterns, or with no pattern at all. It is not for sale and it never will be: the only way to get it is to be one of the first five to finish the collection.

Progression wipes every 6 weeks. The founder colour does not — once it is yours, it stays yours across wipes.

Server rhythm

  • Hosted in Warsaw — low ping across Central and Eastern Europe, Germany and the Nordics
  • Daytime runs at ×6, nights at ×24 — short, but real nights, and you will need a light source
  • Restarts at 02:00, 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00 Kyiv time — 01:00, 07:00, 13:00 and 19:00 CEST. Town loot carries over; what re-rolls at restart is the four paint caches
  • Wipes every 6 weeks, announced in Discord beforehand

Community

Everything happens on our Discord — announcements, wipe dates, paint jobs players show off, promo codes, and the fastest way to reach an admin. English is the server language; Ukrainian is spoken in Discord too.
